How DrainSmart works

1 · SenseWater-level sensors upstream and downstream of each culvert, a rain gauge plus forecast, and optional cameras at trash screens
2 · DetectA digital twin predicts the level a clear drain would show for the rain that fell. Water backing up above that prediction raises the Blockage Index
3 · PrioritiseRisk = blockage likelihood × rain coming in the next 12 h × how close the water is to the bank × acres at stake
4 · AlertSMS / voice alerts in the local language to the village maintenance crew, escalating to the panchayat if nobody accepts the task
5 · ClearSupervised rake at fitted nodes, behind a safety checklist and emergency stop; manual crews everywhere else
6 · VerifyThe task closes only when the sensors show water flowing normally again. If it doesn't, the task reopens automatically

The core signal

When a culvert chokes, water piles up upstream while the downstream level stays flat or falls. Heavy rain raises both sides. DrainSmart compares each sensor against the digital twin's prediction, so a storm doesn't look like a blockage and the crew isn't flooded with false alarms.

Failure cases handled

  • Sensor fouling. A reading that stops moving is flagged as a sensor fault, never as "all clear". The node falls back to its camera, and the system raises a sensor-service task.
  • Partial clearing. If the rake jams and the sensors still show a backup, verification fails and the task reopens for a manual crew.
  • Downstream echo. Water released after an upstream blockage clears can look like a new blockage further down. That is attributed to the upstream node, so no extra alert goes out.
  • No connectivity. Nodes buffer readings and the alert falls back to SMS. In this prototype, the dashboard falls back to a stored forecast if the weather API is unreachable.

What is real and what is simulated in this prototype

  • Real: the rainfall forecast (Open-Meteo), the detection and risk algorithms, the alerting, task and verification workflow, the multilingual alerts and the audit log.
  • Simulated: the water-level and camera readings, generated by a hydraulic model of a 6-node village drain with 131 acres of paddy. The field pilot replaces this with ESP32 + ultrasonic sensor nodes.
